# Bellabeat Case Study: How can Bellabeat play it smart?

Analysis of Fitbit usage data with recommendations for the Bellabeat app. Google Data Analytics capstone project.

**Task:** Find trends in how people use smart fitness devices and suggest how they can inform Bellabeat's marketing strategy. Focus product: Bellabeat app.

**Data:** [FitBit Fitness Tracker Data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arashnic/fitbit) (Kaggle, CC0). 35 users, 12 March – 12 April 2016.

**Tools:** SQL (BigQuery), Google Sheets (charts), Google Slides (presentation).

## Key findings

1. **Activity is the core feature.** All 35 users track activity, 23 track sleep, 14 heart rate and 11 weight.
2. **Most users wear the tracker almost daily.** 21 of 34 users (61,8%) wore it 28–32 of the 32 days; 13 (38,2%) wore it less regularly.
3. **Sleep logging is the least regular habit.** Of 35 users, 12 (34,3%) logged no sleep at all and only 9 (25,7%) logged it almost every night.

## Recommendations

1. **Make sleep logging easier** and deprioritise heart rate and weight. Activity needs no effort, so everyone tracks it; sleep needs effort, so fewer people do.
2. **Use notifications to build the wearing habit:** congratulate users on 3, 7, 14 and 30 days in a row, and show irregular wearers what they gain from regular tracking.
3. **Send an evening sleep reminder** at a time the user chooses, with a short wind-down tip and an easy way to start logging sleep.

Small sample (35 users): pilot each recommendation before rolling it out.

## Limitations

- 35 users, 1 month, data from 2016
- No gender or age data
- Fitbit users, not Bellabeat customers
- Shows behavior, not reasons
